Privacy Policy (NDPR)

Last updated: —

This is a starting template aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). Have a Nigerian lawyer review before publishing.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, hashed password.
  • Listing data: property details, photos you upload.
  • Enquiry data: name, phone, email, message from renters.
  • Technical data: IP address, device, browser, session cookies.

2. Lawful basis

We process data to perform the contract with you (account and listing services), for our legitimate interest in preventing fraud, and with your consent (marketing emails, non-essential cookies).

3. Your rights under NDPR

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data. Email privacy@yourdomain.com to exercise these rights.

4. Data retention

Account data is retained while your account is active plus 12 months. Listing data is retained for 24 months after listing removal for fraud prevention.

5. Third-party processors

We use MongoDB Atlas (database, EU/US region), Render (hosting), Resend (transactional email), and Sentry (error tracking). Each processor is bound by a Data Processing Agreement.

6. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the banner.

7. Cross-border transfers

Some processors operate outside Nigeria. Where data leaves Nigeria we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses permitted under NDPR.

8. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

9. Contact

Data Protection Officer: dpo@yourdomain.com.
