Cookie Policy

Essential cookies

We set two httpOnly cookies to keep you logged in: token (short-lived access token) and refresh (longer-lived refresh token, scoped to /api/auth). These cannot be disabled without breaking login.

Non-essential cookies

We may set analytics cookies (only after you accept them via the consent banner). These help us understand aggregate usage.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies in your browser at any time. You can also withdraw consent via the link in the footer: "Cookie settings".
