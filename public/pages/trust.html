Trust & Safety

What "Reviewed" means

A "Reviewed" badge means a human on our team checked the listing. It is not a guarantee of ownership or condition.

✅ What we check

  • Photos are not obviously reused from elsewhere
  • Price and property type are internally consistent
  • Lister phone number is reachable
  • Description has no prohibited content

❌ What we do NOT do

  • We do not verify ownership documents
  • We do not visit every property
  • We do not guarantee the landlord's identity
  • We do not mediate disputes

Always do your own checks

Before paying any money:

  • Inspect the property in person
  • Ask for the landlord's ID
  • Request proof of ownership (C of O, deed of assignment)
  • Never pay before inspection
  • Never pay into an account that doesn't match the lister's name

Report a listing

Every listing page has a "Report listing" button. Reports go to our moderation queue. You can also email trust@yourdomain.com.
