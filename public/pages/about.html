About Naija Rent & Trust

A demonstration rental marketplace MVP for Nigeria.

Reviewed by NaijaRent. Any "verified" badge in this product is reviewed and exists for demonstration only. It does not represent real-world property verification, ownership checks, or legal due diligence.

No real payments are processed. No real personal data is collected. All sample names, addresses, and prices are fictional.

What this MVP explores

  • Transparent pricing with published fee breakdowns
  • Simple, mobile-first rental discovery
  • An easy reporting channel for renters
  • A lister workspace with listing and enquiry management
