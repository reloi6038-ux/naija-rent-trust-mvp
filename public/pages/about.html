About NaijaRent

A transparent rental marketplace built for Nigeria.

Human-reviewed, not guaranteed. Every listing on NaijaRent is manually checked by our moderation team before it goes live. We verify that photos match the description, pricing is consistent, and the lister's contact details are reachable.

However, we do not verify property ownership, legal titles, or physical condition. Always inspect a property in person and verify documents before making any payments.

What we do

  • Provide transparent fee breakdowns (Rent, Agency, Legal, Caution).
  • Allow renters to submit condition reports after inspection.
  • Maintain a public report system for suspicious listings.
  • Keep the interface lightweight for low-data networks.
